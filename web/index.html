What's new

How Tokentown works

Every Claude Code session on this Mac is a character in the village. Where it stands tells you what it is doing. Click a character to open its session in Claude. A session in VS Code, VS Code Insiders or Cursor, in the Claude Code chat or the editor's own terminal, opens in that editor instead: the window with its folder comes forward, and a chat you have closed there resumes in a new tab. A terminal session that has ended copies its resume command instead: paste it into a terminal.

Where everyone goes

The PorchBlocked sessions, waving under an orange light: they are waiting for you to approve, answer or review a plan. Sessions that finished a turn in the last 2 hours wait here too, as Needs input, with any that errored or stopped mid-turn
The WorkshopSessions working right now, including any still running a background task
The CottagesIdle sessions, open but quiet for 2 hours or more, and Recent ones, closed but active in the past week. Click to look inside, where they play board games
The HarbourSessions with a PR still open on GitHub, queueing behind the border patrol. When the PR merges, the guard stamps their passport and they sail
Valhalla islandSessions whose PR merged, with none still open, or that you sent there. They lie on the beach for 14 days, then move into the sand castle
The JailSessions whose PRs were all closed without merging, for 30 days
The GraveyardArchived chats, terminal and VS Code sessions that have ended, and any with no activity for 30 days
The immigration deskNot sessions: PRs waiting on your review, as visitors with passports. A sash means it was asked of a team you are on. Click one to open the PR
OlderSessions quiet for 7 to 30 days: counted under the pills, not drawn

How a session picks its place

Each session goes to the first of these that fits:

  1. Archived: you put it away, so it rests whatever it was doing, in the Graveyard unless it has a PR still open, or a merge or a done mark to sail on
  2. Blocked: it is waiting for you to approve or answer
  3. Errored: it hit an error recently
  4. Running: it is working, or a background task it started is still going
  5. PR open: one of its PRs is still open, unless you sent it to Valhalla
  6. Valhalla: a PR merged and none is still open, or you sent it there
  7. Needs input: its turn ended in the last 2 hours
  8. Graveyard: it is a terminal or VS Code session that has ended
  9. Jail: its PRs were all closed without merging
  10. Otherwise by how long it has been quiet: Idle, Recent, Graveyard or Older

The board refreshes every couple of seconds. PR states come from GitHub about once a minute, because the Claude app's own record of a PR goes out of date.

A VS Code session never says it is waiting for your approval, so Tokentown works it out: a command that has not started, or an edit not yet made, after 15 seconds is Blocked. A wait on any other tool shows as Running.

Sending a session to Valhalla

Claude never says when a piece of work is finished, so you tell Tokentown. Any of these works, even with a PR still open:

  • Press Send to Valhalla on its card, or pick it and press d
  • Drag its card onto the Valhalla column on the Board
  • Type go to valhalla as a message in the session. It sails once Claude has replied

Bring back undoes it. A session also comes back by itself the moment it does anything new.

Reading a character

  • Colour: the repo it works in. The Repos legend shows which is which
  • Size: bigger means more output tokens, subagents included
  • Badge: its state, in the same colours as the pills at the top
  • Hover for its details, and click to open it in Claude or its editor

Views

  • Village or Board (v): the map, or a board with a column per lane
  • One village or World (w): every repo on one map, or an island per repo. Click an island to sail in, and press Esc to step back out
  • Theme: the green Village, the Wild West or Middle-earth, all on the same map. The frontier town wears cowboy kit over a desert, with a bank, a mine and a locomotive; Middle-earth is a green country of hobbits and dancing ents, with a hobbit hole for a cottage, a spider's lair for a jail, orcs over the barrows and a dark tower on the coast. Every building stands on the footing it replaces, so nothing moves: only the name painted on a board changes
  • Night is dark mode: windows light up, the lighthouse sweeps, ghosts rise over the graveyard, and the sand castle has a disco with fireworks going up outside its windows. In the Wild West the mine has a band, and two of the crowd go at it every few seconds. In Middle-earth the hall has starlight, the tower's Eye sweeps the coast, and the grey pilgrim sends up fireworks over the water, seen again through the hall's own windows
  • The 5-hour and Weekly bars are your Claude plan usage, from the Claude app

What it reads and sends

  • It reads Claude's session files on this Mac, and never changes them: in ~/.claude, in the folder CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR names if you set it, and in the Claude app's own folder. If it finds none of them, or no sessions in them, Health says so
  • A session you start on claude.ai/code runs in the cloud, not on this Mac, so it leaves nothing here and never appears in the village. The Claude app lists those by asking Anthropic for them, which Tokentown does not do
  • It asks GitHub, read-only and through your own gh login, for your PRs' states and the PRs waiting on your review. If gh is not signed in, Health says so: run gh auth login in Terminal
  • About once an hour it asks GitHub the same way whether a new release of Tokentown is out, and reads which version you have from its own git folder. When one is, a banner under the top bar offers What's new, with the release notes, and Update now: it updates with git, only ever as a fast-forward, and restarts Tokentown. It never updates without that click, and Later hides the banner until the next release. Health names your version
  • Nothing else leaves your Mac, and it only answers on this Mac (127.0.0.1)
  • The only files it writes are its own, in Application Support: a secret, your Valhalla marks and the PR links it has seen
  • Press p to hide titles before you share your screen. A Privacy on button shows in the top bar while they are hidden: click it to show them again
